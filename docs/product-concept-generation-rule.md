# Product concept generation rule

Fruma's potential end-product visual must be generated from reconciled approved product intent, not treated as an open-ended creative redesign.

Flow: search / designer brief -> sketch + technical pack -> conflict and gap reconciliation -> approved product specification -> faithful AI visual interpretation -> physical sample -> validated product truth.

The AI visual must not invent unsupported trims, colours, branding, construction or styling. Unresolved details stay neutral or are flagged. The visual remains AI interpretation until physically validated.
